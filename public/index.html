Homes

Users

Everyone in this organization. New users sign in with a magic link and join with the organization code above; their role starts as employee.

Rate table

Dollars per mile by effective date. The rate in effect on a trip’s date applies to that trip. Rates are history — past rates can’t be changed or deleted, because past trips depend on them.

CareMiles is a record-keeping tool, not legal advice. Mileage reimbursement rules (including California Labor Code 2802) are the employer’s responsibility — the app records the miles; it does not decide the law.
